Why My Lot exists

A normal subscription starts when the coffee is already finished. My Lot starts where coffee starts.

Most coffee companies can tell you a story about origin. Very few can bring you into the actual chain of decisions that created the coffee. They buy green coffee. Or they roast. Or they retail. They enter the process late.

Colita works upstream. Our work spans origin relationships, coffee selection, fermentation and processing, drying, quality, movement through the supply chain, roasting and the final drinking experience. That is why My Lot can be immersive rather than theatrical.

You can only build a true origin-to-cup membership if you are present at every critical stage between the tree and the cup. We are.

The unfair advantage

We do not bolt “experience” onto coffee.

The experience exists because the supply chain exists beneath it. Every stage gives the member something real to see, understand, influence or learn from.

Origin & crop

You begin with a defined origin relationship, not an anonymous warehouse product. The farm, the environment and the crop are part of the membership story from day one.

Harvest

You follow the point where flavour potential is still alive in the fruit: ripeness, picking and the decisions made before processing begins.

Fermentation & processing

This is where Colita's specialist work matters. Washed, natural, honey, anaerobic and other controlled processing directions are not labels for us — they are tools for designing flavour.

Drying & quality

The process does not end when fermentation stops. Drying, stability and quality control preserve or destroy what was built upstream.

Milling, export & movement

Your coffee remains connected to its identity as it moves through the supply chain instead of becoming just another anonymous commodity lot.

Roasting

The roast reveals what origin and fermentation created. Members learn how roast direction changes expression instead of treating “light” or “dark” as a random preference toggle.

Brewing & drinking

The final stage belongs to you. Monthly coffee becomes the practical lesson: brew it, taste it, compare it and understand why it tastes the way it does.

Questions

What My Lot actually means.

The point is clarity. The proposition should be easy to explain in one minute and still have enough depth to explore for two years.

Is My Lot just a coffee subscription?

No. The recurring coffee is the physical backbone of the membership, but the proposition is the origin-to-cup experience around it: origin connection, processing, fermentation, roast, education, traceability and physical immersion.

How much coffee do I receive?

The membership is built around 24 kg across 24 months — one kilogram per month.

What does the £900 cover?

It covers the 24-month My Lot membership and its 24 kg coffee allocation. The value proposition is not presented as commodity coffee alone; it is the combination of coffee with the immersive origin-to-cup experience.

Why can Colita offer this when most coffee brands cannot?

Because My Lot depends on real involvement upstream and downstream. Colita's model is built around origin relationships, fermentation and processing, quality, movement through the supply chain, roasting and the final cup. Without that chain, the “experience” would be marketing rather than access.

Can I learn about fermentation and roasting?

Yes. That is a core part of the proposition. My Lot is designed to help members understand how processing and roast choices alter flavour instead of merely reading those words on packaging.

Is there a Colombia experience?

Origin immersion is part of the My Lot vision: giving members a route to experience the land, people and production environment behind their coffee rather than keeping origin permanently behind a screen.
